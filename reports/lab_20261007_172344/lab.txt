  news blackout     no new trade on a jobs / CPI / Fed day until 60 minutes after the release
  confidence gate   each lane learns, from its OWN past trades only, which entry reads have gone with its results
                    (80+ trades, 2.5+ standard errors). While the account is 1%+ below its high, a signal whose
                    proven reads say 'below average' stays home. Soft: it never acts at a new high.

  recovery scouts   while 1%+ below the high, the 2 best-proven lanes go out at 2x minimum size, one at a time
  harvest           a week that earns 0.25%+ of the floor banks 25% of its profit, if equity stays above the floor
