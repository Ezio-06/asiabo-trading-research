exit and costs)   t (of the edge).  OLD = 2014-21 (2014-17, 2018-19, 2020-21), NEW = 2024+.  2022-23 sealed.
+ = net R/trade above 0 in BOTH old and new.  E = edge above 0 in both with t >= 2 in at least one.
There are about 90 cells and many share the same trades: a few will show |t| above 2.5 by chance. A cell
counts only if it holds in both periods AND on more than one market (see the by-market lines).
Swing2 / rsi2 control = the same trade held over the same time in the other direction (their exits are not
mirror images). TESTED on a pure random walk before shipping: edges came out at 0 within noise.
Exits:
  orig   as recorded (the lane's own target / clock)
  close  no target, out at the session close
  24h    no target, out after 24 trading hours
  48h    no target, out after 48 trading hours
  trail  stop trails at the trade's own risk distance behind the best close (48h cap)
  5d     chandelier: 1 daily ATR behind the best close, up to 5 trading days
  2R     the old way: 2R target, else out at the session close
  own    the lane's own exit rule
