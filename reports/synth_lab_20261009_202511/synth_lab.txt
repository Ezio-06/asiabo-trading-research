counts only if it holds in both periods AND on more than one market (see the by-market lines).

mirror images). TESTED on a pure random walk before shipping: edges came out at 0 within noise.

  5d     chandelier: 1 daily ATR behind the best close, up to 5 trading days

  own    the lane's own exit rule
