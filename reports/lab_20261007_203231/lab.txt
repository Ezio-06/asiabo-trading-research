PART 7: PROBE   what price does after the moments the reads point at (no lane's stop or target involved)
====================================================================================================
The lanes' own stops and targets cost 0.06-0.17R a trade, about as much as the reads are worth. So this part
drops them: it takes each MOMENT, enters at the close of that bar in the stated direction, and measures the raw move.
Moments (fixed before measuring):
  1 bad fade           a fade fires with 4+ of its 5 bad signs -> go WITH the move it is fading
  2 fade stopped       a fade lane's trade is stopped out -> go WITH the move that stopped it
  3 clean breakout     a breakout fires with none of its 3 bad signs -> go WITH the breakout
  4 bad breakout       a breakout fires with all 3 bad signs -> go AGAINST the breakout
  5 strong momentum    a momentum trade fires with all 5 good signs -> go WITH it
  base: any fade       every fade entry (the comparison for 1) -> go AGAINST the fade
  base: any breakout   every breakout entry (the comparison for 3 and 4) -> go WITH the breakout
  base: any momentum   every momentum entry (the comparison for 5) -> go WITH it
  base: random         the control lane's random entries -> its random direction
bad signs of a fade: day_pos_with low, trends agree, far target, few tests of the level, choppy last 60 days.
bad signs of a breakout: trends disagree, no failed fade against it, far target.
good signs of a momentum trade: a failed fade against it, straight day, with the day's move, with the 1-hour trend, stretched.
One moment per market, kind and direction every 2 hours. ATR = M15 ATR(14) at the moment.
LONG HOLDS: hours here are TRADING hours (bars), so 24h is about one trading day and crosses the nightly break;
weekends are crossed too. Swap is charged for every night held (your sheet's figure, scaled to the price then).
COSTS: REAL = your broker's commission + the spread bars.db recorded at that bar (gold's commission is assumed).
For 'fade stopped' the entry is the close of the M15 bar in which the stop was hit, so the stop-out move itself is
already over and is not counted.
