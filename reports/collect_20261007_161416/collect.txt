==============================================================================
DATA COLLECTION  --  every lane in shadow, what was known at entry, what happened after
==============================================================================
Nothing here decides anything. It measures, on the two TRAINING periods only.
Sealed exam years 2022-01-01 .. 2024-01-01: not read by this mode, whatever EXAM_UNLOCK says.
Lanes: the bot's 16 + swing (20-day breakout, trailing exit) + dip (pullback in a trend) + control.
US 500 now runs on ITS OWN clock (New York cash session), not gold's hours: see the CLOCK lines under
its heading. Gold is untouched: same clock, same trades as the previous collection.
NEW MARKETS (EURUSD, USDJPY, XTIUSD = WTI, GERMANY40): each on its own clock with its own costs from its symbol sheet;
see the COSTS and CLOCK lines under each heading. FX and Germany 40 use the European clock (London open = lane 07:00),
WTI the New York clock. Japan 225 is left out on purpose (Tokyo-session data only until Oct 2024, a 4-year hole).
Changed since the first collection: scalp's stop is the far end of its impulse bar (was the midpoint);
swing has no 20-day cap (trailing exit only); session lanes close at the day's last bar if the data ends
early. New sections 9-11: stop menu, entry delay, cost rule. Sections 1-8 are otherwise as before.
DATA QUALITY (new): bars.db's hole list decides which days and trades a hole touches. Section 0 shows what was
cut and why, 1b/1c count trading days and trades taken per lane and per regime. Bars read from 2020-01-01 on
(COLLECT_FROM, or the start date you gave). Cutting rule: 120+ unexplained minutes; CLEAN_ONLY=True.
