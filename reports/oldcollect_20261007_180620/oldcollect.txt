==============================================================================
DATA COLLECTION  --  every lane in shadow, what was known at entry, what happened after
==============================================================================
Nothing here decides anything. It measures, on the two TRAINING periods only.
Sealed exam years 2022-01-01 .. 2024-01-01: not read by this mode, whatever EXAM_UNLOCK says.
Lanes: the bot's 16 + swing (20-day breakout, trailing exit) + dip (pullback in a trend) + control.
US 500 now runs on ITS OWN clock (New York cash session), not gold's hours: see the CLOCK lines under
its heading. Gold is untouched: same clock, same trades as the previous collection.
NEW MARKETS (EURUSD, USDJPY, XTIUSD = WTI, GERMANY40): each on its own clock with its own costs from its symbol sheet;
see the COSTS and CLOCK lines under each heading. FX and Germany 40 use the European clock (London open = lane 07:00),
WTI the New York clock. Japan 225 is left out on purpose (Tokyo-session data only until Oct 2024, a 4-year hole).
Changed since the first collection: scalp's stop is the far end of its impulse bar (was the midpoint);
swing has no 20-day cap (trailing exit only); session lanes close at the day's last bar if the data ends
early. New sections 9-11: stop menu, entry delay, cost rule. Sections 1-8 are otherwise as before.
DATA QUALITY (new): bars.db's hole list decides which days and trades a hole touches. Section 0 shows what was
cut and why, 1b/1c count trading days and trades taken per lane and per regime. Bars read from 2014-01-01 on
(COLLECT_FROM, or the start date you gave). Cutting rule: 120+ unexplained minutes; CLEAN_ONLY=True.

2. SIGNALS WITH A PREDICTED DIRECTION  (top third minus bottom third of the signal, in R)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PASS = z >= 1.0 in EACH period AND combined z >= 2.0. z is already signed so that
  positive = the way theory said. A signal that works the opposite way is a fail, not a find.

  breakout
    setup_range_rel  not enough trades in one of the periods   (setup range small vs recent daily ranges (quiet before expansion))
    trend20_with     not enough trades in one of the periods   (break goes with the 20-day trend)
    open_hours       not enough trades in one of the periods   (break in the London/New York open hours)
    bar_extreme      not enough trades in one of the periods   (breakout bar closes near its extreme)
    room_r           not enough trades in one of the periods   (room before yesterday's high/low)

  reversal
    er20             not enough trades in one of the periods   (recent days made little net progress)
    trend20_with     not enough trades in one of the periods   (trade goes with the 20-day trend, not against it)
    stretch_atr      not enough trades in one of the periods   (bigger stretch from fair value (VWAP))
    hour             not enough trades in one of the periods   (later in the session)

  momentum
    trend20_with     not enough trades in one of the periods   (intraday lean matches the 20-day trend)
    day_used         not enough trades in one of the periods   (day has NOT already used most of its usual range)
    volrank          not enough trades in one of the periods   (volatility high against its own history)

  swing
    er20             not enough trades in one of the periods   (market has been moving in a straight line)

  13 signals tested. By luck alone, expect about 0.2 of them to pass.
